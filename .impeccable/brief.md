# Portfolio surface brief

Scope: single-page portfolio (app/page.tsx). Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters + tech peers, 30-second scan.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a mixtape made for the visitor; the J-card tracklist is both navigation and summary. Refuses the dark-neon hero + three-card dev template.
OWN-WORLD: Cobalt desk ground (drenched), cool-white J-card stock, ink-black print, fluoro orange label stamps, highlighter yellow for the single active mark. Rows, ruled lines, stamped caps, Sharpie annotations used sparingly; clear cassette shell with live reels.
STORY: In seconds the visitor learns Juan Diego builds unusual, current-tech software, then sees proof (projects), people (voices), and personality (beyond code), and reaches out.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: sticky left J-card (cassette with turning reels above the tracklist, Side A / Side B) beside a right column with the name at display scale, role, tagline, three featured work rows and contact links. Primary action: Projects track + email.
FORM: Mixtape J-Card, candidate 3 of the re-rolled grounded list; seed:2c73f527b07c80b7.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Revision (bolder pass)
Cassette, Side A/B and cobalt/orange palette retired at the owner's request. Now: opaque gray-green desk, wing-mark logo, plain contents rail with a segmented 'page loaded' progress bar, photo placeholders (polaroid), certificate slots, and light 2000s-web touches (old-OS window frame, marquee ticker, pixel sparkle/heart GIFs, 88x31 badges) in the 'Too online since 2012' section and small accents only.
