# Portfolio surface brief

Scope: single-page portfolio (app/page.tsx). Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters + tech peers, 30-second scan. Primary sections: About, Projects, Experience. Techs and Testimonials stay short.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page itself is the navigation: no sidebar, the scroll carries the story and a bottom dock tells you where you are. Refuses the fixed-rail-plus-stacked-sheets template it replaces.
OWN-WORLD: Established green world kept (desk ground, pale card sheets, ink, fern label, lime highlighter, Bricolage + JetBrains Mono + Permanent Marker, light/dark). Large 28px sheets, pill controls, polaroid-tape photos; bento tiles in card, sage and ink.
STORY: Visitor meets Juan full-width (name, role, CV, socials), sees the person through a photo bento, then proof: featured projects and a timeline that fills as you scroll, then short techs and voices, academic, archive, contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width hero: lang/theme top right, name at display scale left, role, tagline, pitch, actions (See projects, Download CV), social icon row (Email, LinkedIn, GitHub); tilted polaroid right. Dock floats bottom center.
FORM: Scroll-driven single column with floating bottom dock; user-specified structure inside the established world, so no concept roll was run.
seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the bottom dock expands the active section's label as you scroll; sections reveal with view-timeline motion; the experience rail fills with scroll progress.
